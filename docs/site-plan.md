# Site plan

What Dave wants searenityonlummi.com to be, gathered by interviewing him. Update this as decisions are made.

## Scope

- The site is about **Searenity on Lummi**, a property on Lummi Island, Washington, co-owned by Dave, his sisters Trish and Verna, and his wife Sharon.
- The property is where friends and family gather: weekends, holidays, Thanksgiving, and many other occasions.
- **Migley** is the adjoining property, owned by Dave and Sharon. The septic file names mention both properties (to confirm with Dave: does the new system serve both?).
- Planned sections:
  1. **Calendar**
  2. **Favorite pictures**
  3. **Property logistics**
  4. **Projects**: the new septic system is the first. More to come, such as a fence and a beach trail.

## Audience

- Mainly friends and family who come to the property. Tone can be warm and personal; projects told as stories more than technical manuals.

## Calendar

- The family already keeps a Google Calendar of scheduled activity at Searenity.
- Showing it on the public site would require making the calendar public, which tells anyone when the house is empty. To decide: link to the private calendar, or embed it publicly.

## Property logistics

- A map of how to get there.
- Light and heat instructions.
- Garbage instructions.
- How to leave the place (closing-up checklist).
- Keep off the public site: door or lockbox codes, Wi-Fi passwords, alarm details. To decide: whether the map shows the house itself (which publishes its location) or only the route to the island and ferry.

## Open questions

- Calendar: private link or public embed.
- Septic: does the new system serve both Searenity and Migley?
- Map: house location or only the general route.
