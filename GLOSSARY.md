# Glossary

**Searenity on Lummi**: the Loeppky family home on Lummi Island, Washington, where Dave Loeppky lives. The name of the place, not of the website.

**Dave**: Dave Loeppky, owner of Searenity on Lummi, author of the site's content, and the person driving the agent day to day.

**Steve**: Dave's son, who set up the domain and hosting and helps when Dave gets stuck.

**Site**: the public website at searenityonlummi.com. Its scope (the place as a whole, or only Dave's projects) is Dave's to decide.

**Originals**: Dave's full-size photos, videos, and source documents (such as the Pages writeup and its PDF export). They stay on Dave's Mac and are never published; the site uses smaller, cleaned copies.

**Publish**: make Dave's current version of the site live at searenityonlummi.com. Only happens when Dave says so.
