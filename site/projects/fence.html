The new fence

Replacing the old fence at Searenity.

Section 1

Background

The existing fence was installed before we bought Searenity in 2015. It wasn’t in the best of shape, but it was straight and solid. It was also brown!

It was beginning to fail in places. The driveway gate was rotten and would not open and close properly, and it kept on decaying. Rotten fence boards would blow out in high winds; they were put back with baler twine and duct tape! The header boards were sagging. And a large cedar tree had grown to the point where it had lifted the fence and pushed it toward the road.

More of the fence story is coming soon.
