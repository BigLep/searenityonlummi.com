WiFi and TV

Internet

Select the network Searenity. For the password, ask Dave.

Living room TV (LG remote)

  • On/off: the “ON/OFF” button.
  • Volume: the “+ −” buttons.
  • Netflix, Amazon, Apple TV and the rest: the “House” button.

Fubo TV

Press the “House” button and select Fubo. Use the outer ring of the center circle (up, down, left, right) to work through the guide.

Deck speakers

Connect your phone by Bluetooth to PDA20BT. Your phone needs to be inside, in the living room or kitchen, to connect; it won't connect from outside the house.

Bedroom TV

Use the TV remote for everything. Only Canadian stations are available.
